About

I am Amit Kumar Singh, a Research Scholar (Ph.D.) in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at Motilal Nehru National Institute of Technology (MNNIT), Allahabad, India, under the supervision of Prof. Mayank Pandey. I also serve as an Assistant Professor in the School of Engineering and Technology at K.R. Mangalam University, Gurugram, India. My research focuses on Software-Defined Networking (SDN), Programmable Data Planes (P4), In-Band Network Telemetry (INT), Network Automation, Intent-Based Networking, AI/ML for Network Management, and Future Internet Architectures. My work aims to design intelligent and efficient network management frameworks that provide real-time visibility while minimizing telemetry overhead and preserving high forwarding performance. I am particularly interested in bridging the gap between programmable networking and artificial intelligence to develop adaptive, scalable, and self-managing network infrastructures. My current research explores application-aware telemetry, selective and event-driven telemetry mechanisms, and AI-assisted decision-making within programmable network devices. Over the years, I have authored and presented research papers in international conferences and journals covering programmable networks, network telemetry, and intelligent network management. My research emphasizes both theoretical foundations and practical implementations using technologies such as P4, BMv2, Mininet, ONOS, P4Runtime, and modern machine learning frameworks. Alongside my research, I am passionate about teaching and mentoring undergraduate and postgraduate students. I teach courses in Software Engineering, Computer Networks, Data Analytics, Operating Systems, and related areas, with a strong emphasis on experiential learning and hands-on laboratory practice. I actively guide student projects and encourage research-oriented thinking in emerging networking technologies. My long-term vision is to contribute to the development of autonomous, intelligent, and programmable communication networks capable of meeting the demands of next-generation computing environments, including cloud computing, edge computing, 6G, and AI-native networking. I welcome opportunities for academic collaboration, industrial research partnerships, joint publications, and student supervision in the areas of programmable networks, network telemetry, and intelligent network management.
